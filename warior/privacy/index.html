Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Warior ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Warior mobile app, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Warior, we may collect:

  • Profile information — name (or anonymous label), battle front category, and motivation style you choose during onboarding.
  • Generated content — motivational phrases created for your feed, including labels, quotes, highlights, and authors.
  • Usage data — app interactions such as missions completed, streaks, and level progress.
  • Device information — basic technical data (device type, OS version, app version) needed to run and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Personalize your home feed with motivational phrases tailored to your profile.
  • Store your missions, progress, and streak data.
  • Improve app performance, features, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests if you contact us.

3. AI-Generated Content

Warior uses Google Gemini AI to generate motivational phrases based on your selected battle front and motivation style. Your profile details may be sent to Gemini solely to produce this content. Generated phrases are saved in our database and displayed in the app.

We do not use your data to train public AI models. AI processing is limited to delivering personalized content within the app.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers using industry-standard practices. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and restrict access to authorized systems only. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g. cloud hosting, AI generation).
  • When required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.
  • With your explicit consent.

6. Your Choices

You can:

  • Use the app anonymously without providing a real name.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.
  • Stop using the app at any time and uninstall it from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Warior is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may link to or integrate with third-party services (such as Google Gemini). Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
