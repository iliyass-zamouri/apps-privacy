Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hailsom ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Chameleon DIY mobile application (also called Spot the Chameleon; package com.hailsom.chameleonirl).

Information Collection and Use

Chameleon DIY is a photo hide-and-seek game. You hide a chameleon in a photo; other players hunt it. To run that service we collect the following.

Account and device identifiers. You play as a guest. We create a player profile (player ID, display name, username) tied to a device identifier stored on your device. We may also store the IP address used when the account is created or last used, for abuse prevention. Optional Google or Apple sign-in may be offered in future updates; those providers process authentication under their own policies.

Photos and puzzles you publish. When you create a puzzle we upload the original photo, the composite image (chameleon hidden in the photo), chameleon pose/placement data, and optionally an edit timeline used for timelapse playback. Published composites appear in the public feed. Original photos and timelines are stored so we can serve gameplay, moderation, and timelapse features.

Gameplay. We store attempts (found or not, time, miss count), puzzle reports, and profile stats such as puzzles created or found. Liked and saved puzzle IDs are stored locally on your device.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The app shows interstitial ads via Google AdMob. Ads may use a device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple Identifier for Advertisers) to deliver personalized ads, subject to your consent and platform settings.

In regions that require it (for example the EEA, UK, and Switzerland) we present a consent form through Google’s User Messaging Platform. On iOS we may also request App Tracking Transparency permission. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Non-personalized ads may still be shown if you decline personalization.

Analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications

We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand feature use (for example puzzle opened, published, or solved) and may associate events with your player ID.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes. Crash reports can include device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces.

If you allow notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so we can send alerts (for example when a puzzle you created goes live). You can disable notifications in system settings. Tokens are removed if the related player record is deleted.

Third-Party Services

Chameleon DIY uses Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play services) and Apple (App Store, push, optional future sign-in) to distribute, operate, advertise, and improve the app. Those services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

How we use information

  • Operate the feed, hunt gameplay, profiles, and timelapse playback
  • Moderate user-generated photos and handle reports
  • Show ads and measure that they were displayed
  • Improve reliability and understand which features are used
  • Send optional push notifications you have allowed
  • Prevent abuse (for example duplicate guest accounts or spam reports)

Data Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to servers is restricted to authorized operators. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Uninstalling the app removes local data (device ID, likes, saves) but does not by itself delete photos or the guest profile stored on our servers.

Your Choices

You can refuse personalized ads via the in-app consent form and device privacy settings. You can turn off notifications at any time. To request access to or deletion of your account, published photos, and related server data, contact us at the email below and include your in-app username or player ID if you have it.

Children’s Privacy

Chameleon DIY is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
