01 Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Hailsom (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the BPP mobile and web application (“Service”) — a football prediction and league competition product (package com.hailsom.bpp).

By using BPP, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

02 Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive identifiers such as your name, email address, and authentication tokens provided by that provider.
  • Profile & gameplay data. Display name, predictions, scores, league memberships, invite codes you create or join, and standings related to your activity.
  • Device & usage data. App version, device type / OS, language, crash logs, and basic analytics needed to operate and improve BPP.
  • Communications. Messages you send us (support requests, feedback).
We do not ask for payment card numbers inside BPP for core prediction play. If billing is added later, this Policy will be updated before collection begins.

03 How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account and leagues
  • Record predictions, compute scores, and show standings
  • Authenticate you via Google or Apple Sign-In
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests
  • Monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and improve the Service
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information.

04 Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Other users — display name, predictions (as shown in-app), and league standings are visible to members of leagues you join.
  • Service providers — hosting, authentication, analytics, and crash reporting vendors who process data only to provide services to us.
  • Auth providers — Google and Apple process sign-in under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal — when required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of users and Hailsom.

05 Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When you delete your account (or request deletion), we remove or anonymize associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

06 Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also revoke Google or Apple access from your provider account settings.
